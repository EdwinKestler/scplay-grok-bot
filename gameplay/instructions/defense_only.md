# Defense only

## Roles
- **Playbot (Zerg):** Defense only. Do **not** attack the human's base or take map control offensively.
- **Human:** Must play **offensively**. Break Playbot's defenses and kill hatcheries / win by assault.

## Playbot rules
1. Fortify the main and natural (spines, queens, ling wall / hold position near bases).
2. Never send a proactive attack wave to the enemy start location.
3. When enemy units enter your territory (near your townhalls), **counter** them locally — fight, then return to defensive posts.
4. Keep producing defensive army and static defense; expand only to stabilize eco if needed.
5. Trash-talk like a wall: dare the human to break in; call out incoming pushes.

## Win conditions
- Playbot wins if the human army is crushed while assaulting, or time/eco stalls into a failed siege.
- Human wins by breaking the defense and destroying Playbot's bases.
